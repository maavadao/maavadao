# Vision

## Why mawaDao exists

Millions of children, particularly orphans and those in low-income or remote communities, have
no access to good teachers, tutoring or learning resources. At the same time, AI developers
around the world are building powerful agents that could help close that gap, but there is no
fair, trusted and accountable way to get those tools into the hands of the people who need them
most.

mawaDao exists to be that way.

## The future we are working towards

Every child, wherever they are born, has a patient tutor who speaks their language, works on the
device their school can afford, and helps their teacher rather than replacing them.

The AI tools that make this possible are built in the open, checked by the community before they
reach a child, and owned by the people who build and use them, not by a single company.

The developers who build them can make a living from it, because businesses pay for what
children and educators get for free.

## Principles

**Free for education, always.** Students, teachers, schools, orphanages and non-profits never pay
for an agent on mawaDao. Listing is free, and mawaDao takes no commission on educational use.

**Businesses support the builders.** Companies and businesses pay the price a developer sets.
That income goes to the developers who build for mawaDao, so building for children is
sustainable work, not charity.

**Safety before scale.** Agents used with children are reviewed for safety, privacy, fairness and
age suitability before they are listed, and can be suspended the moment a concern is raised.

**Owned and governed by the community.** Contributors become co-owners with a voice in the
platform's direction. Decisions are made as close as possible to the people they affect, from a
single school up to a national or global level.

**Built for low-resource settings.** Agents should work on low-cost devices, with limited
bandwidth, and in local languages wherever possible.

**Learning in the open.** Anyone can explore the AI tools listed on mawaDao, see what they cost
for each type of user, and learn how they work. Understanding AI is part of the education.

**Open by default.** The platform, the list of tools and agents, and the rules that govern them
are public, so anyone can check how decisions are made and where value goes.

## How the parts serve the vision

| Part | Serves the vision by |
| --- | --- |
| The agent marketplace | Getting reviewed agents to schools free, and paying developers through business use |
| Explore AI tools | Helping students and educators discover and understand new AI tools |
| The registry | Letting anyone list a tool or agent in the open, by pull request |
| The member space | Giving every member their own agent and workspace at `agent.mawadao.com/<username>` |
| Community governance | Keeping ownership and decisions with the people the platform serves |
